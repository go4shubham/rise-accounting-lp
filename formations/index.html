Frequently Asked Questions

What is company formation?
Company formation is the process of legally registering a business as a limited company with Companies House. Once registered, your company becomes a separate legal entity with its own tax obligations and limited liability protection for shareholders.
What details do I need to provide to incorporate my business?
You'll need a proposed company name, a UK-registered office address, at least one director (name, date of birth, address, nationality, occupation), at least one shareholder, share capital structure and details of any Persons of Significant Control. We collect all of this in a jargon-free flow on the platform.
What is a limited company?
A limited company is a business structure where the company is legally separate from its owners. The company has its own finances, pays its own taxes and its shareholders' personal assets are protected if the business gets into debt.
What if I change my mind?
You can cancel your order at any point before your details are submitted to Companies House. Once Companies House has processed the incorporation, the company legally exists and you would need to formally dissolve it. We can guide you through either route.
Do I have to incorporate as a limited company?
No. You can trade as a sole trader or partnership. A limited company gives you liability protection, potential tax efficiency and a more credible structure when raising investment or dealing with larger clients. Most founders we work with choose to incorporate for those reasons.
Do I need to submit an ID or proof of address?
Yes. UK anti-money-laundering rules mean we verify the identity and address of every director and Person of Significant Control. Our platform handles this via a quick digital ID check.
Do I need a business bank account?
A limited company legally needs its own bank account (separate from your personal account). We include a free business bank account of your choice with every package. Tide, Barclays, Wise, ANNA, Mettle and Revolut are all supported.
How much does it cost?
